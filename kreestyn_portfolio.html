I'm a builder. I build strategies, brands, presentations, and systems — always asking: is this clear, is this useful, and does it actually move something forward?

I'm a Management major concentrating in Entrepreneurial Studies at Texas State, and I genuinely love the place where business strategy and creative execution overlap. I'm someone who understands why a business needs to communicate something and knows how to make that communication actually land — whether that's a brand strategy deck, a consulting deliverable, a product case study, or a content system someone can run without me.

Right now I'm working as an Underwriting Assistant Intern at United States Liability Insurance, where I process 100+ policies weekly, coordinate with underwriters and agents, and analyze production reports in Excel to support team execution. I also founded YAHS Descent, a women's wellness and lifestyle brand that won $10,000 in the Campus 2 Community Pitch Competition. Those two experiences — one inside a structured corporate environment, one building from nothing — have shaped how I think: with precision, an eye for process, and a real bias toward outcomes.

I lead social media strategy for NABA's Texas State chapter, where I grew account reach by 1,700% in 30 days and increased event attendance by 25%. I design the graphics, write the copy, build the strategy, and track the results. All of it, every week.
